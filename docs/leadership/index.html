03

Strategic vision

The user-owned network
The thesis

When you can’t own the land, the app store, or your social graph — the next ownable thing is the network itself.

Echo is already built on the three primitives needed to issue that ownership to users instead of renting it to them: a hard-capped token, content-blind stakeable relay infrastructure, and portable on-chain identity.

Economic

Fee-split, not ad-extraction

Relay, anchoring, and payment-rail fees split on-chain back to users, validators, and the Foundation — the platform itself never extracts value.

Infrastructure

Content-blind, community-run

A relay that cannot read messages has no platform moat. Anyone can run a relay or validator, stake ECHO, and earn — toward sovereign edge and mesh relays.

Governance

Your stake is your equity

A staking-gated citizenship credential bundles security deposit, governance vote, and revenue claim into one portable, un-revocable asset.

Load-bearing milestone

Validator federation (Phase 4+) — what turns “Echo the app” into “Echo the network you own a piece of.”

Steward

Echo Protocol Foundation (Wyoming DUNA) + a Privacy Commons Treasury for legal defense of surveilled users.

Token posture at launch

ECHO exists (1B hard cap) but is deferred & value-free — no sale, no “buy,” no APY marketing to consumers pre-counsel.

Consumer messaging · Phase 2

Echo Message

Phone-free, passkey-secured, end-to-end messaging with verifiable did:key identity, trust tiers, no ads or tracking, and optional earned ECHO rewards. Signal-grade crypto — with identity that travels with you.

01

What it does

Encryption

Double-ratchet E2EE, sealed-sender, post-quantum hybrid bootstrap, and an optional Tor / SOCKS transport.

Conversations

1:1 and groups with live typing, receipts and reactions; voice notes; a WebRTC voice/video baseline.

Identity & trust

did:key identity, passkeys, and five-level trust tiers — no phone number or email required.

Discovery

Private set-intersection contacts, @username, profile QR, and time-limited echo://invite links; multi-device link-QR.

Message controls

Disappearing, edit, delete and pins; local search and archive; a content-blind relay that only sees ciphertext.

Wallet

Balance, stake and claim messaging rewards — earn, not buy. Messages can be anchored to Data L1 for proof.

Enterprise compliance · Phase 7

Echo Comply

Secure, cryptographically verifiable messaging for regulated industries — end-to-end encrypted team communication with built-in retention, tamper-evident audit trails, eDiscovery export, and integrity proofs anchored to a public ledger, without exposing message content to any server, chain, or administrator.

Service + web portal shippedPublic launch after Sept 1
01

What it does

Retention & holds

Configurable retention (permanent / time-limited / litigation hold) with custodian scoping and a five-second custodian-notify SLA.

eDiscovery

Export by matter, custodian set and date range; privileged threads excluded by default, overrides audit-logged.

Web-primary portal

Admin dashboards for evidence coverage, hold counts, exports and anchor health — the repo’s first web build target.

Integrity proofs

Compliance commitments anchored to Echo Data L1 — hashes and CIDs only, never content.

Governance

On-chain org-role credentials (owner / admin / moderator / member); SSO / SAML / SCIM on the roadmap.

Vertical reports

Segment dashboards for HIPAA, FOIA and law-firm postures — aggregate metrics, exportable to PDF / JSON.

Load-bearing invariant

Zero readable PII, no server-held key. The operator portal renders only hashes, CIDs, and aggregate metrics — never message content. That’s both the privacy guarantee and the enterprise defense.

Verifiable identity & payments · Phase 2→5

Echo Passport

The holder-side wallet, selective-disclosure, and agentic-presentation layer over credentials Echo mostly already produces — not a new PII database. Hold identity, payment, and employment credentials, and use them to access services and transact.

Holder core shippediOS module + rails next
01

What it does

Holder vault

Client-encrypted credential sync to IPFS / Storj — Echo holds ciphertext + CID only, never plaintext keys.

Selective disclosure

SD-JWT presentations reveal the minimum claim — “over 21” — never the source document.

Recovery

Shamir M-of-N social / threshold recovery, designed with guardians rather than a server honeypot.

Consent primitives

On-chain AllowSpend / SpendTransaction — single-use, amount-capped, short-TTL authority.

Credential types

Proof-of-Humanity, KYC-Lite, High-Assurance, Professional, Device-Attestation, ISO 18013-5 mDL.

Payment wire

Adopts x402 (HTTP 402) layered on AllowSpend; agentic grants via GNAP — no standing approval, ever.

Locked design principles
  • Hybrid custody — no server-held key ever; that’s the honeypot line.
  • Confirm-per-action — biometric gate; agents and glasses never hold standing authority.
  • Never the money transmitter — Echo is the identity + consent layer only.
  • Never a raw PAN — a credential attests “verified Visa ending 1234.”
  • Holder, not issuer — Echo holds credentials; it does not issue government ID.
  • Reuse, don’t rebuild — a wallet over credentials Echo already mints.
