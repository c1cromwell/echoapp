## What ECHO Networks LLC actually needs
1. **Business checking** for operating funds, strictly separate from personal money (protects your
   liability shield — `COMPANY_POLICIES_DRAFT.md` Policy 1).
2. **A business credit or charge card** for cloud/SaaS/marketing spend, with controls and rewards.
3. **Tech/startup-friendly onboarding** (no minimum revenue, fast online opening, QuickBooks/accounting
   integration for clean books).
4. **A forward path for the token/payments roadmap** — but note: that track is **not active today** and
   is legally gated (§7), and **most fintechs restrict crypto/MSB activity** (see the caveat at the end).

## ⚠️ The crypto/MSB caveat (read before choosing)
The neobank/fintech options below are excellent for **normal operating banking**, but they generally
**prohibit money-services-business (MSB) / crypto-exchange activity** in their terms. That's fine now —
Echo's token is value-free at launch and any payments are designed to be **non-custodial** so Echo is not
an MSB (§7). But if a future feature ever makes Echo transmit or custody funds, you'll need (a) a **FinCEN
MSB registration + state money-transmitter licensing**, and (b) a **specialized banking relationship**
that serves lawful MSBs. Do **not** run crypto/MSB activity through a fintech account that prohibits it —
that risks abrupt closure. Clear any such feature with money-transmission counsel first.

## The five recommendations

### 1. Mercury — *default pick for a tech startup*
- **Why it fits:** built for startups; no minimum revenue/balance; fast online opening; broad FDIC sweep
  (spreads deposits across many partner banks for coverage far above the standard $250K); strong
  accounting integrations; treasury option as you build a balance.
- **Card:** the **IO** charge card (physical + virtual, spend controls, cashback) — pay-in-full charge
  card rather than a revolving line.
- **Watch-outs:** it's a fintech (deposits held at partner banks, not a bank itself); **crypto/MSB
  activity is restricted** — operating banking only.

### 2. Brex — *strongest corporate card + spend controls*
- **Why it fits:** high-limit corporate card with excellent spend management, virtual cards, and
  bill-pay; good for scaling software/marketing spend; startup-oriented onboarding.
- **Card:** the Brex corporate card is its centerpiece (dynamic limits, controls, integrations).
- **Watch-outs:** **Capital One completed its acquisition of Brex in April 2026** — the product
  continues, but confirm current terms/eligibility as the roadmap integrates. Historically Brex has
  leaned toward funded/scaling startups; **crypto/MSB restricted**.

### 3. Bluevine — *true business checking + credit line*
- **Why it fits:** an actual business checking account with a competitive APY on balances (Premier tier),
  plus access to a **business line of credit** — useful if you want borrowing capacity alongside
  checking. Good middle ground between a neobank and a traditional bank.
- **Card:** checking + line of credit; pair with a separate business card if you want richer rewards.
- **Watch-outs:** verify APY tier requirements; **crypto/MSB restricted**.

### 4. Relay — *multi-account structure for clean, no-commingling books*
- **Why it fits:** lets you open **multiple accounts** to organize cash by purpose (operating, taxes,
  reserves) — directly supports the separateness/recordkeeping discipline that protects your LLC shield;
  clean QuickBooks/Xero sync; no minimums.
- **Card:** Relay Visa debit cards across accounts (good control); pair with Brex/IO for a charge card if
  you want a credit product.
- **Watch-outs:** fintech (partner-bank deposits); **crypto/MSB restricted**.

### 5. Grasshopper (or Axos) — *tech-friendly bank to anchor the relationship / token track*
- **Why it fits:** **Grasshopper** is an FDIC-insured **digital bank** (not just a fintech) built for
  startups/VC, with free business checking, competitive APY, and a business card — a good "real bank"
  anchor. **Axos** is a full-service online bank (checking/savings/treasury/lending, ATM cash deposits)
  if you want broader banking services under one roof.
- **Card:** business credit/debit card options; confirm current card program.
- **Watch-outs:** still **not** general crypto/MSB banks — for actual lawful MSB/crypto activity later,
  look at providers that explicitly serve MSBs (e.g., **Old Glory Bank**'s MSB/crypto program, or the
  new OCC-chartered crypto trust banks) **with counsel**, and keep that separate from operating banking.

## Suggested setup
- **Primary operating + card:** **Mercury** (or **Brex** if you want the stronger corporate card),
  opened with the organizational consent's banking resolution (`ORGANIZATIONAL_CONSENT_DRAFT.md` §7).
- **Books discipline:** consider **Relay** (or Mercury sub-accounts) to keep taxes/reserves separated.
- **Bank anchor:** optionally add **Grasshopper/Axos** as a chartered-bank relationship.
- **Do NOT** route any future crypto/MSB activity through the above — stand up a specialized, licensed
  relationship for that track when/if counsel clears it (§7).
