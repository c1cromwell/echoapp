## Article 1 — Formation

1.1 **Formation.** The Company was formed as a Wyoming limited liability company by the filing of
Articles of Organization with the Wyoming Secretary of State, effective on the date stated above, under
the Wyoming Limited Liability Company Act `[VERIFY § — Wyo. Stat. Ann. §17-29-101 et seq.]` (the "Act").
This Operating Agreement (the "Agreement") governs the Company and, to the extent permitted, controls
over any non-mandatory provision of the Act.

1.2 **Name.** The name of the Company is **ECHO Networks LLC**. The Company may operate under registered
trade names (DBAs), including without limitation **Echo Message**, **Echo Comply**, and **Echo
Passport**, as authorized by the Manager.

1.3 **Purpose.** The Company is formed for any lawful purpose, including developing and operating the
Echo privacy protocol and its products, owning and licensing intellectual property, and holding assets.
Nothing in this purpose authorizes any activity (token distribution, custodial payments, HIPAA-regulated
services) that the Company's compliance plan defers to licensed counsel.

1.4 **Term.** Perpetual, until dissolved under Article 9.

1.5 **Registered agent / office.** The Company shall continuously maintain a Wyoming registered agent and
registered office as stated above, and a principal office that is **not** the Member's personal
residence.

1.6 **Single-member intent.** The Company presently has one Member. The parties intend that the Company
be respected as an entity separate from the Member, and that the Member's liability be limited as
provided by the Act. Wyoming extends charging-order protection to single-member LLCs `[VERIFY § —
Wyo. Stat. Ann. §17-29-503]`; the Member intends to preserve that protection by observing the
separateness covenants in Article 8.

## Article 2 — Member, Units, and Capital

2.1 **Sole Member.** The sole Member is the person named in the fill-in box, holding **100%** of the
membership interests, represented by the number of **Membership Units** ("Units") stated above. Units
are personal property; no Member has any interest in specific Company assets.

2.2 **Capital contribution.** The Member's initial capital contribution is the amount and/or property
described in the fill-in box, which includes the intellectual property conveyed under the **IP
Assignment Agreement** (`IP_ASSIGNMENT_AGREEMENT_DRAFT.md`). The Member is not obligated to make
additional contributions.

2.3 **Capital accounts.** The Company shall maintain a capital account for the Member consistent with
applicable tax rules; the Manager and the Company's CPA shall determine the mechanics.

2.4 **No interest; limited liability.** No interest accrues on capital contributions. The Member is not
personally liable for the Company's debts, obligations, or liabilities solely by reason of being a
Member or Manager, except as required by the Act.

2.5 **Additional Units / new Members.** The Company may authorize and issue additional Units and admit
new Members only as provided in Article 7 and the **Membership Interest Issuance Guide**
(`MEMBERSHIP_INTEREST_ISSUANCE_GUIDE.md`). Until then, the Member holds all Units.

## Article 3 — Management (Manager-Managed)

3.1 **Manager-managed.** The Company is **manager-managed**. Management is vested exclusively in the
Manager; the Member, in its capacity as Member, does not manage or bind the Company. This structure keeps
the Member off the public record and lets the Manager be the operational face.

3.2 **Initial Manager.** The initial Manager is the person or entity named in the fill-in box. The
Manager may be an individual (you) or a separate **manager entity** you control, for additional distance
between your name and the Company's public dealings.

3.3 **Powers.** The Manager has full authority to conduct the Company's business, including to: open and
operate bank accounts and designate signers; enter, sign, and perform contracts; register DBAs/trade
names; acquire, license, and dispose of property and intellectual property; hire and engage personnel and
contractors; make tax elections (Article 6); and take any other action consistent with this Agreement and
the Act. Third parties may rely on the Manager's authority to bind the Company.

3.4 **Signatory authority.** Any document, contract, banking resolution, or filing signed by the Manager
on behalf of ECHO Networks LLC binds the Company. The Company, not the Member individually, is the party
to all Company contracts.

3.5 **Standard of conduct.** The Manager shall act in good faith and in the Company's best interests. To
the fullest extent permitted by the Act, fiduciary duties are limited/modified as the parties may agree,
and the Manager is not liable for good-faith business judgments `[VERIFY § — Wyo. Stat. Ann.
§17-29-409/§17-29-110 permissible modifications]`.

3.6 **Removal / resignation / vacancy.** The Manager may be removed or replaced, and vacancies filled, by
the Member. A resigning Manager shall give reasonable written notice.

3.7 **Compensation.** The Manager may be compensated as approved by the Member and reflected in the
**Founder Services Agreement** (`FOUNDER_SERVICES_AGREEMENT_DRAFT.md`).

## Article 4 — Indemnification

4.1 The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless the Manager and the Member, to the fullest extent
permitted by the Act, from liabilities incurred in connection with Company business, except for conduct
constituting fraud, willful misconduct, or a knowing violation of law. The Company may advance expenses
and purchase D&O/management-liability insurance.

## Article 5 — Distributions

5.1 **Discretionary distributions.** The Manager may cause the Company to make distributions to the
Member from available cash, subject to the Act's solvency limits `[VERIFY § — Wyo. Stat. Ann.
§17-29-405]` and prudent reserves. As a single-member company, all distributions go to the Member.

5.2 **Tax distributions.** Because the Company is by default a disregarded entity (Article 6), the Member
should reserve for taxes on Company income; the Manager may make distributions sufficient to cover the
Member's estimated tax liability attributable to the Company.

5.3 **No commingling.** Distributions must be recorded and paid from the Company account to the Member's
personal account — never by paying personal expenses directly from Company funds (see Article 8).

## Article 6 — Tax

6.1 **Default classification.** So long as the Company has one Member, it is a **disregarded entity** for
US federal income tax; the Company's income and loss are reported on the Member's personal return.

6.2 **Reserved elections.** The Manager, with the Company's CPA, may elect: **S-corporation** treatment
(Form 2553) when profit warrants; and/or, upon admission of additional Members, **partnership** treatment
by default or a corporate election. See `CORPORATE_STRUCTURE_AND_COMPLIANCE.md` §9.

6.3 **Fiscal year.** The Company's fiscal year is `[FILL IN: calendar year, unless CPA advises otherwise]`.

6.4 **Books; separateness for tax.** The Company shall keep books adequate for its tax classification and
file all required Wyoming annual reports and license-tax filings.

## Article 7 — Transfers, New Members, and Death

7.1 **Transfer restrictions.** The Member may not transfer, pledge, or encumber Units except (a) to a
revocable living trust or estate-planning vehicle the Member controls, (b) as part of admitting a new
Member under this Article, or (c) as the Member otherwise directs in writing. A transferee acquires only
economic rights (a "transferable interest") unless admitted as a Member.

7.2 **Admission of new Members.** New Members may be admitted, and additional Units issued, by the
Manager with the Member's written consent, following the mechanics in the **Membership Interest Issuance
Guide** (ledger update, unit certificate, admission joinder, capital contribution or profits-interest
grant, and CPA/valuation review). Upon admission of a second Member, this Agreement shall be amended to
add multi-member governance and the tax classification revisited (Article 6).

7.3 **Transfer-on-death / estate plan.** The Member may designate a successor (including a revocable
living trust naming the Member's intended beneficiary) to receive the Units on the Member's death,
avoiding probate where permitted. Absent a designation, the Units pass under the Member's estate plan or
applicable law. This provision is intended to work with, not replace, a separate estate plan prepared by
an estate attorney (see `CORPORATE_STRUCTURE_AND_COMPLIANCE.md` §11). **Note:** gifting or transferring
an interest may have gift-tax and valuation consequences — coordinate with the CPA/estate attorney.

7.4 **No dissolution on transfer.** A permitted transfer or the admission of a Member does not dissolve
the Company.

## Article 8 — Separateness & Liability-Shield Covenants

To preserve the Member's limited liability and Wyoming's charging-order protection, the Company shall:

8.1 Maintain its **own bank accounts**; never commingle Company and personal funds.
8.2 Keep **separate books and records** and observe the recordkeeping policy
(`COMPANY_POLICIES_DRAFT.md`).
8.3 Contract, hold property, and hold IP **in the Company's name** (ECHO Networks LLC), not the Member's.
8.4 Adequately capitalize the Company for its activities and pay Company obligations from Company funds.
8.5 Document material decisions by **written consent** (`ORGANIZATIONAL_CONSENT_DRAFT.md` and successors).
8.6 Hold itself out to the public as ECHO Networks LLC (or a registered DBA), and sign as the Company.

## Article 9 — Dissolution & Winding Up

9.1 The Company dissolves upon the Member's written election, or as required by the Act. On dissolution,
the Manager winds up the business, pays or provides for liabilities, and distributes remaining assets to
the Member (or successor). Articles of Dissolution shall be filed with Wyoming.

## Article 10 — General Provisions

10.1 **Governing law.** Wyoming law governs this Agreement, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules.
10.2 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended by written consent of the Member.
10.3 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, together with the IP Assignment, Founder Services Agreement,
company policies, and organizational consents, is the entire agreement regarding the Company's internal
affairs, and supersedes prior understandings.
10.4 **Severability.** If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.
10.5 **Counterparts / electronic signature.** May be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
10.6 **Interpretation.** Section headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without
limitation."
