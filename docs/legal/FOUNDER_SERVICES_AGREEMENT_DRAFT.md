## 1. Engagement & Services
1.1 The Company engages the Founder to provide services as its Founder and Manager, including product and
protocol development, strategy, operations, and such other services as the Company requires (the
"Services").

1.2 The Founder will perform the Services in good faith and consistent with the Operating Agreement
(`OPERATING_AGREEMENT_DRAFT.md`) and Company policies (`COMPANY_POLICIES_DRAFT.md`).

## 2. Term
2.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either party on
`[FILL IN: e.g., 30 days']` written notice, or as the parties otherwise agree. Sections 4–7 survive
termination.

## 3. Compensation
3.1 The Founder's compensation is as stated in the fill-in box. While the Company is a **disregarded
entity**, amounts paid to the Founder are generally owner draws/distributions rather than wages; upon an
**S-corp election**, the Founder's compensation will be restructured into a reasonable salary plus
distributions per the CPA's guidance (`CORPORATE_STRUCTURE_AND_COMPLIANCE.md` §9).

3.2 The Company reimburses reasonable, documented business expenses under the expense policy — never by
the Founder paying Company costs personally without reimbursement, to preserve separateness.

## 4. Ownership of Work Product (Going-Forward IP Assignment) — key provision
4.1 **Present assignment.** The Founder **hereby assigns to the Company**, effective on creation, all
right, title, and interest in and to all inventions, code, works of authorship, designs, documentation,
trade secrets, know-how, and other intellectual property that the Founder creates, conceives, or reduces
to practice **in the course of or relating to the Services or the Company's business** (collectively,
"Work Product"). Work Product is owned by the Company as it comes into existence.

4.2 **Work made for hire.** To the extent Work Product qualifies as "work made for hire," it is the
Company's; to the extent it does not, it is assigned under §4.1.

4.3 **Moral rights.** The Founder waives moral rights in the Work Product to the fullest extent permitted
by law.

4.4 **Further assurances / power of attorney.** The Founder will sign documents and take actions to
perfect the Company's ownership, and appoints the Company as attorney-in-fact to do so if the Founder is
unavailable (coupled with an interest, irrevocable).

4.5 **Prior IP.** Intellectual property created before the Effective Date is transferred under the
separate `IP_ASSIGNMENT_AGREEMENT_DRAFT.md`; together, §4 and that agreement cover past **and** future IP
with no gap.

## 5. Confidentiality
5.1 The Founder will hold the Company's confidential information in confidence, use it only for the
Company's benefit, and not disclose it except as authorized or required by law. This obligation survives
termination.

## 6. Return of Materials
6.1 On termination, the Founder will return or, at the Company's direction, securely destroy Company
materials and confidential information in the Founder's possession, except as retained to comply with law.

## 7. General
7.1 **Governing law.** Wyoming. 7.2 **Relationship.** Nothing here makes the Founder a partner of, or (for
now) a W-2 employee of, any third party; the Founder acts for the Company. 7.3 **Assignment.** The
Company may assign this Agreement to a successor; the Founder may not assign it. 7.4 **Entire agreement /
amendment.** Entire agreement on its subject; amendable only in a signed writing. 7.5 **Counterparts /
e-signature.** Permitted. 7.6 **Severability.** Unenforceable provisions are severed.
