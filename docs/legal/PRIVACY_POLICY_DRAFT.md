## 1. Our privacy approach
ECHO is built for data minimization: no phone number or email is required to use the app, and your
messages are end-to-end encrypted so we cannot read them.

## 2. Information we collect
- **Account:** a cryptographic public identifier (DID) created on your device. No phone number/email required to use ECHO.
- **Identity verification:** when you verify, our third-party provider (`[IDV PROVIDER]`) processes your
  government ID and/or selfie/liveness. We receive a **verification result/assurance level**, not your
  raw documents (handled under the provider's policy: `[IDV PROVIDER PRIVACY URL]`).
- **Waitlist (marketing):** if you join, the email or social handle you submit, plus an optional
  referral code, used solely to contact you about access. Unsubscribe anytime.
- **Device & usage:** limited technical data (app version, device type, diagnostics, IP for security/
  anti-abuse) as described here.
- **Payments (if used):** processed by third parties; we receive limited transaction metadata, not card data.

## 3. What we do NOT collect
- **Message content** — end-to-end encrypted; we cannot read it.
- **Raw identity documents** — held by the verification provider, not ECHO.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. How we use information

waitlist/referrals, comply with law, and improve reliability.
