## Anonymity reality (so you don't waste effort)

- ✅ **Public record / users / App Store:** your name stays hidden; the **entity** is the public face.
- ❌ **Your bank (KYC) and the IRS (EIN):** you disclose your real identity. Required. Don't try to hide
  it — that's where legal anonymity ends.
- ⚠️ **Don't put the company in your son's name to hide yourself** — it's not real anonymity, can void
  your liability shield, and creates gift-tax/creditor risk. Use Wyoming privacy + (optionally) a
  revocable trust instead. See §4.1 of the master doc.

## Do NOT do yet (talk to counsel first — §7, §12)

- ❌ Sell/list/distribute the ECHO token, or advertise "APY"/yield/returns.
- ❌ Turn on any custodial payment feature.
- ❌ Sign a HIPAA BAA (Comply) before you're actually HIPAA-ready.
- ❌ Form the Foundation before the token is genuinely moving toward distribution.
