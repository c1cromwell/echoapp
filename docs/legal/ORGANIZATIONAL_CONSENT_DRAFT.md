The undersigned, being the **sole Member** and the **initial Manager** of ECHO Networks LLC (the
"Company"), acting by written consent in lieu of an organizational meeting as permitted by the Wyoming
Limited Liability Company Act and the Company's Operating Agreement, hereby adopts the following
resolutions, effective as of the date above.

## 1. Ratification of Formation
**RESOLVED**, that the formation of the Company by the filing of Articles of Organization with the
Wyoming Secretary of State (filing number and date in the fill-in box) is **ratified, confirmed, and
approved**, and that all acts of the organizer and registered agent in connection with formation are
ratified.

**RESOLVED**, that the **Operating Agreement** of the Company (`OPERATING_AGREEMENT_DRAFT.md`, as
finalized) is hereby **adopted** as the governing agreement of the Company, and the Member's execution of

**RESOLVED**, that `[FILL IN: your legal name / manager entity]` is appointed as the **initial Manager**

**RESOLVED**, that the application for and receipt of the Company's federal Employer Identification Number

## 5. Acceptance of the IP Assignment
**RESOLVED**, that the Company **accepts the assignment** of all intellectual property described in the
**IP Assignment Agreement** (`IP_ASSIGNMENT_AGREEMENT_DRAFT.md`) from the Member/Founder to the Company,

recorded as the Member's initial capital contribution and Company property, and that the Manager is
authorized to record and perfect the Company's ownership (trademark/copyright recordals, domain

## 7. Banking Resolutions
**RESOLVED**, that the Manager is authorized to **open one or more business bank and card accounts** in
