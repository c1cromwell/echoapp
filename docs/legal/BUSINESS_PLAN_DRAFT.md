## 1. Executive summary
ECHO Networks LLC operates **Echo**, a privacy-first communications and identity protocol delivered as
three products under one brand: **Echo Message** (consumer end-to-end encrypted messaging), **Echo
Comply** (enterprise compliance, audit, and eDiscovery messaging), and **Echo Passport** (a credential
wallet with selective disclosure and in-chat payments). The Company owns all underlying intellectual
property and runs the products as divisions/DBAs, spinning out subsidiaries only when specific business
triggers require it.

**Mission:** give people private, verifiable communication and identity that they — not surveillance
intermediaries — control.

## 3. Products
1. **Echo Message** — consumer E2E-encrypted messaging (1:1 and group), content-blind by design, with
   trust-tier and abuse-reporting primitives. Revenue: subscriptions / VIP features.
2. **Echo Comply** — enterprise-grade compliance messaging: audit trails, eDiscovery, retention, and
   legal-hold controls, storing hashes/metrics rather than message content. Highest-value, highest-
   assurance product; first subsidiary spin-out candidate. Revenue: enterprise SaaS contracts.
3. **Echo Passport** — credential wallet with selective disclosure and (planned, counsel-gated)
   non-custodial in-chat payments; Echo is the identity + consent layer, never the money transmitter.
   Revenue: verification and rails partnerships.

*(Protocol/token layer — the ECHO token and "Network State" governance — is pre-architected but
**deferred and value-free at launch**, and any distribution is gated on securities counsel per
`CORPORATE_STRUCTURE_AND_COMPLIANCE.md` §7. It is not part of current revenue.)*

## 4. Market & differentiation
- **Market:** secure messaging and digital-identity, spanning consumer privacy, regulated-enterprise
  compliance messaging, and verifiable credentials.
- **Differentiator:** a single privacy protocol that is **content-blind by architecture** yet
  **compliance-capable** for enterprises, plus user-held identity — combining guarantees usually split
  across separate consumer, enterprise, and identity vendors.
- **Competitive context:** positioned against mainstream messengers and point compliance/IDV tools; the
  privacy-plus-verifiability combination is the wedge (`CORPORATE_STRUCTURE_AND_COMPLIANCE.md` sources).

## 5. Business model & revenue
- **Consumer:** freemium + subscription/VIP (Echo Message).
- **Enterprise:** annual SaaS contracts, seats/usage, with security and compliance add-ons (Echo Comply).
- **Identity/payments:** verification and partner-rail revenue (Echo Passport), non-custodial by design.
- **Cost base:** cloud/infrastructure, IDV and processor partners, engineering, security/compliance,
  and legal/insurance.
