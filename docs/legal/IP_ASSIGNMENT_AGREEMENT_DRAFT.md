## Recitals

A. The Assignor is the founder and sole Member of the Company and, prior to and during formation,
independently created software, brand assets, documentation, and other intellectual property relating to
the Echo privacy protocol and its products (Echo Message, Echo Comply, Echo Passport, and the ECHO
token/protocol materials).

B. The Company was formed to own and operate this intellectual property. The parties intend that **all**
such intellectual property be owned by the Company, effective as of the date above.

C. This Agreement documents the transfer as part of the Assignor's capital contribution and/or for the
consideration stated above.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Assigned IP"** means all intellectual property and proprietary rights, worldwide, whether
registered or unregistered, that the Assignor owns or has rights in as of the Effective Date relating to
Echo, including all items listed in the Exhibits and all of the following:

- (a) **Software & code** — the "ECHO Code": all source and object code, repositories, the iOS app
  (Echo), backend/metagraph services, scripts, build tooling, configuration, and related technical
  materials (Exhibit A);
- (b) **Copyrights** — all works of authorship, including code, documentation, designs, UI/UX, screen
  catalog assets, written specs, and marketing copy;
- (c) **Trademarks & brand** — the **"ECHO" word mark**, the ripple/soundwave logo, and the product
  names **Echo Message**, **Echo Comply**, **Echo Passport**, together with associated goodwill
  (Exhibit B);
- (d) **Domains & handles** — all internet domain names, social-media handles, and app-store identifiers
  (Exhibit C);
- (e) **Trade secrets & know-how** — algorithms, cryptographic designs, protocol designs, non-public
  technical and business information;
- (f) **Patents** — any inventions, patent applications, and patentable subject matter, if any;
- (g) **Third-party/contractor deliverables** — all right, title, and interest the Assignor holds in work
  created by contractors or third parties and assigned to or owned by the Assignor (Exhibit D);
- (h) all **registrations, applications, renewals, and rights to sue** for past, present, and future
  infringement, and all income and proceeds relating to the foregoing.

1.2 **"Excluded IP"** means only the items, if any, expressly listed in **Exhibit E**. If Exhibit E is
blank, there is no Excluded IP.

## 2. Assignment

2.1 **Present assignment.** The Assignor hereby irrevocably **assigns, transfers, and conveys to the
Company all right, title, and interest** in and to the Assigned IP, effective as of the Effective Date,
free of liens and encumbrances except as disclosed. This is a present transfer, not an agreement to
transfer later.

2.2 **Copyright / work made for hire.** To the extent any Assigned IP qualifies as a "work made for
hire," it is owned by the Company; to the extent it does not, it is assigned under Section 2.1.

2.3 **No retained license.** The Assignor retains no license to the Assigned IP except any use expressly
authorized by the Company in writing (e.g., in the Founder Services Agreement while performing services
for the Company).

## 3. Moral Rights Waiver

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Assignor waives and agrees not to assert any **moral rights**
(rights of attribution and integrity) in the Assigned IP against the Company and its licensees and
successors.

## 4. Further Assurances

4.1 The Assignor will, at the Company's request and expense, sign any documents and take any actions
reasonably necessary to **perfect, record, and enforce** the Company's ownership (including trademark and
copyright assignment recordals, domain transfers, and patent assignments).

4.2 **Power of attorney.** If the Assignor is unable or unwilling to sign such documents, the Assignor
appoints the Company (and its Manager) as attorney-in-fact to execute them, this power being coupled with
an interest and irrevocable.

## 5. Representations & Warranties

The Assignor represents that: (a) the Assignor is the owner of the Assigned IP and has the right to assign
it; (b) to the Assignor's knowledge, the Assigned IP does not infringe third-party rights, except as
disclosed; (c) the Assigned IP is not subject to any conflicting assignment, lien, or license except as
disclosed in the Exhibits; and (d) any open-source components incorporated in the ECHO Code are used in
compliance with their licenses, an inventory of which the Company will maintain
(`CORPORATE_STRUCTURE_AND_COMPLIANCE.md` §10.2).

## 6. Consideration

The assignment is made in consideration of the membership Units issued to the Assignor as the Company's
sole Member and/or the nominal cash amount stated in the fill-in box, receipt and sufficiency of which
are acknowledged. The parties intend the contribution of the Assigned IP to be treated as part of the
Assignor's capital contribution for tax purposes; the Company's CPA shall determine the tax treatment and
basis. (Contributing appreciated IP can have tax consequences — confirm with the CPA before signing.)

## 7. General

7.1 **Governing law.** Wyoming. 7.2 **Entire agreement / amendment.** This Agreement (with its Exhibits)
is the entire agreement on its subject and may be amended only in a signed writing. 7.3 **Successors.**
Binds and benefits the parties' successors and assigns. 7.4 **Counterparts / e-signature.** Permitted.
7.5 **Severability.** Unenforceable provisions are severed.
