# ECHO vs Radar.chat — Battlecard (1-page, for PR & community reps)

**Full plan:** [`ECHO_VS_RADAR.md`](ECHO_VS_RADAR.md) · **Voice/claims:** [`ECHO_CONTENT_PACK.md`](ECHO_CONTENT_PACK.md)
Keep it factual. Market only what ships. Never say "unhackable / send real money / worth $."

---

## One line
> **Radar is a Bitcoin wallet that added chat. ECHO is a private network you own.**
> Radar: *spend bitcoin like a text.* ECHO: *own the network you chat on.*

## Who Radar is (respect it)
Signal-Protocol messenger + real self-custodial **Bitcoin Lightning** payments (Breez SDK).
Ex-Cake Wallet founder; funds Signal; iOS-first. **Real strength:** send real BTC in chat
today. **We do not claim payment parity — we change the axis.**

## Why ECHO (lead with these — all shipped)
1. **No phone number, no email** — your device key (Face ID) is your identity.
2. **Verified humans, not bots** — trust tiers + verified-human badge; flags impersonators.
   *(A payment app can't stop the scammer who texts you. Identity can.)*
3. **Messages you can prove** — on-chain commitments + merkle proof. No messenger does this.
4. **Post-quantum-hybrid encryption** — future-proofed, today.
5. **Own it, don't rent it** — no ads, no data harvesting, community-governed. *(Sovereignty
   — NOT a token-value/equity claim.)*
6. *(Coming, beta-frame only)* **Works off-grid** over Bluetooth mesh — Radar is online-only.

## Objection handling
- **"But Radar sends real Bitcoin."** → "Great if your goal is spending bitcoin. ECHO's goal
  is a private life you own — verified humans, no phone number, provable messages, works when
  the network doesn't. Owning the network beats renting a payment rail."
- **"Isn't Radar more legit? Cake Wallet founder, funds Signal."** → "Radar's a solid Bitcoin
  app. Different job. We're not a wallet with chat — we're private, scam-free comms you own,
  for people who don't want a crypto tutorial to message a friend."
- **"Do I need crypto to use ECHO?"** → "No. Open app → Face ID → you're in. No number, no
  seed phrase, no bitcoin required."

## Do / Don't
✅ Punch at the category limit (spend-vs-own, crypto-literacy barrier, no anti-scam/identity).
✅ Concede Radar's payments win, then pivot the axis.
🚫 Don't attack Signal or Bitcoin holders (Radar funds Signal; privacy fans skew pro-BTC).
🚫 Don't claim ECHO payments, token value, mesh-as-shipped, or "more secure than Signal."
