## TL;DR — the counter-positioning

> **Radar is a Bitcoin wallet that added chat. ECHO is a private network you own.**
> Radar: *spend bitcoin like a text.* ECHO: *own the network you chat on* — verified
> humans, no phone number, and messages you can prove.

**Don't fight Radar on payments.** Radar has real, spendable Bitcoin in chat *today*; ECHO
launches its token value-free by design. Change the axis: compete on **verifiable trust +
sovereignty on top of strong privacy**, where Radar has nothing.

## 1) Who Radar is (be honest — credibility matters)

- E2E messenger (Signal Protocol, built independently) + **self-custodial Bitcoin Lightning
  payments** (Breez SDK). Founder **Vikrant Sharma** (Cake Wallet). Radar financially
  supports Signal. Launched early July 2026, iOS-first (Android on roadmap).
- **Real strength — say it plainly:** you can send real Bitcoin inside a chat, self-custody,
  instant. Proven components, credible Bitcoin founder. **We do not claim payment parity.**
- **Structural gaps we attack:** Bitcoin-only (single volatile asset, ~$5k Lightning-
  liquidity ceiling in testing, no fiat on/off-ramp, needs crypto literacy); Signal-style
  account identity (no self-sovereign DID); **no anti-scam / verified-human layer**; **no
  provable/anchored messages**; **no off-network mode**; classic (non-post-quantum) crypto;
  no communities and **no ownership** — on Radar you *spend*, you never *own*.

## 2) Strategic frame

- **Category reframe:** the fight isn't "messaging + payments" (Radar wins "payments
  today"). It's *"a payment rail you rent" vs "a private network you own."*
- **Audience:** Radar → Bitcoin holders (niche). ECHO → scam-weary mainstream +
  privacy-conscious + crypto-curious who want **ownership without volatility**. We
  deliberately cede the Bitcoin-maximalist crowd and win the far larger pool.
- **Emotional wedge:** Radar optimizes *spending*. ECHO optimizes *safety + sovereignty*:
  no scammers, no phone number, nothing to leak, works when the network doesn't.

1. **"Own vs Spend" explainer** — thread + short video reframing the category. **Week 7–8**

3. **"No Bitcoin degree required"** — accessibility contrast: Lightning liquidity / seed

   the non-crypto majority Radar structurally can't reach.
4. **Off-grid teaser (beta-framed)** — "works when the network doesn't"; Radar is online-only.
   **Week 5–6** (offline mesh hero — already beta-framed). Real recorded demo only.

## 6) What NOT to do

- **Don't attack Signal or Bitcoin/BTC holders.** ECHO reveres Signal; Radar funds Signal;
  many privacy fans are pro-Bitcoin. Punch at the *category limitation* (spend vs own,
  crypto-literacy barrier, no anti-scam/identity), never the tech or the people.
- **Don't claim payment parity, real token value, or "own = equity worth money."** Keep
  "own" framed as sovereignty (own your identity/data, no ads, community-governed) — the
  token-value/ownership-as-investment narrative is **counsel-gated** (see the tokenomics
  launch posture); a value promise here is exactly the securities risk we're avoiding.
- **Don't claim mesh or in-chat payments as shipped**, and never "more secure than Signal"
  or "unhackable."
- **Don't out-Bitcoin Radar.** Move the axis; don't chase it.
