Ripple Pulse — Pure Mark

The solid core represents the origin of a message. Three concentric inner rings build resonance outward from it. Asymmetric ripple arcs expand in all directions — top, bottom, and sides — evoking the propagation of an echo signal. The graduated opacity creates natural depth and motion without animation.

Trademark Notes
This mark is an original geometric construction: a solid core surrounded by concentric rings with asymmetric ripple arcs radiating outward.
The combination of full concentric rings at center + broken arcs at the perimeter is distinctive in the messaging/tech category.
Works in full color, monochrome, and reversed — required for USPTO/EUIPO registration.
