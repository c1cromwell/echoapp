Echo Product Identity

Comply & Passport Logo Concepts

Each product logo uses the ECHO Ripple Pulse mark as its foundation, with a distinctive overlay symbol and color palette to differentiate the product line while maintaining brand coherence.

Echo Comply

Organization compliance dashboard. Color: Emerald green (#10B981) — trust, verification, regulatory confidence. Each concept layers a compliance symbol over the ripple pulse base.

Concept 01
Shield & Check
Compliance shield with verification checkmark at center. Ripple arcs fade behind the shield, conveying protected signal propagation. Direct, institutional.
Concept 02
Seal of Balance
Official circular seal framing scales of justice. The outer ring doubles as the ripple boundary, blending the echo pattern with a regulatory emblem. Authoritative, legal.
Concept 03
Audit Clipboard
Checklist clipboard at the ripple core. Each completed audit item radiates outward through the echo arcs. Practical, process-oriented, immediately readable as "compliance."
Concept 04
Identity Fingerprint
Ripple arcs morph into fingerprint whorls around the core. A visual pun: the echo IS the identity signature. Dual green tones create depth. Biometric, forensic, unmistakable.
Concept 05
Locked Pulse
The full echo ripple recolored in emerald, with a padlock integrated at the core. The ripple radiates FROM the lock. Simplest variant: color shift + one strong symbol. Secure, institutional.

Echo Passport

Verifiable credentials wallet. Color: Violet (#8B5CF6) — digital identity, trust, credential authority. Each concept layers a credential/identity symbol over the ripple pulse base.

Concept 01
Verified Credential
ID card with chip and verified badge floating at the ripple center. The credential itself becomes the signal origin. Modern, clean, immediately reads as "digital ID."
Concept 02
Skeleton Key
Cryptographic key placed vertically through the ripple center. The key head ring aligns with the inner concentric circles. Signals access, authority, and control over one's credentials.
Concept 03
Digital Passport
Physical passport silhouette with the ripple mark as its central emblem. The most literal interpretation: your digital passport, branded with the echo signal.
Concept 04
QR Portal
QR code corner brackets frame the ripple center, with abstract data blocks replacing inner rings. The ripple becomes scannable data. Tech-forward, modern, bridges physical and digital credential presentation.
Concept 05
Global Identity
Globe at the ripple center with a person silhouette beneath. Your identity, recognized worldwide. The echo arcs become latitude lines at the poles. Grand, aspirational, human-centered.
Design Notes
All concepts use the ECHO Ripple Pulse mark as their foundation layer.
Comply palette: Emerald #10B981 / #34D399 — trust, regulation, go-signal green.
Passport palette: Violet #8B5CF6 / #A78BFA — digital authority, premium credential space.
Each concept works in monochrome (stroke-only) and at app-icon scale (40px+).
