Echo Comply

Minimal Overlay Concepts

Each concept keeps the full ECHO Ripple Pulse mark mostly intact in emerald, with a small, precise overlay that signals compliance. Same philosophy as Passport's QR Portal: modify, don't replace.

Concept 01
Check Core
The center dot is replaced by a single checkmark. Smallest possible change — the ripple radiates from a verified origin. Everything else stays identical to Messaging.
Concept 02
Dashed Ring
One inner ring becomes dashed — a single stroke-dasharray change. Subtly evokes measurement, audit segmentation, regulatory checkpoints. Almost invisible at a glance, distinct on inspection.
Concept 03
Inspection Brackets
Four corner brackets frame the full ripple — "under review." Same bracket language as Passport's QR Portal, adapted for compliance. The pulse is being examined, validated, approved.
Concept 04
Shield Boundary
The outermost ripple arcs are replaced by a single faint shield outline. The inner pulse stays identical. The shield doesn't dominate — it's a perimeter, a containment boundary.
Concept 05
Gauge Ticks
Eight small tick marks at cardinal and diagonal positions — like a compass or measurement gauge. The ripple becomes a calibrated instrument. Precision, metrics, monitoring.
Concept 06
Double Check
Two overlapping checkmarks at center — "verified AND compliant." Echoes the double-tick pattern from messaging read receipts. Dual-tone green adds depth. Simple, immediately understood.
Concept 07
Hex Ring
One concentric ring becomes a hexagon — structured, crystalline, engineered. Suggests blockchain/hash verification while keeping the organic ripple arcs. One shape swap, maximum differentiation.
Concept 08
Crosshair
Four thin alignment lines extend from the outer arcs toward center, stopping before the inner rings. Registration marks — precision printing, forensic overlay. The ripple is being measured.
Concept 09
Approval Stamp
Full ripple mark untouched, with a small circular approval stamp in the lower-right corner. Like a notary seal on a document. The ripple is the signal; the stamp is the certification.
Concept 10
Segmented Arcs
The outer arc pair is split into three segments each — gaps create a "progress bar" or phased-reporting feel. Everything else identical. The compliance signal arrives in stages, checkpoints, phases.
Design Direction
These concepts range from nearly invisible (02 Dashed Ring, 08 Crosshair) to moderately visible (01 Check Core, 09 Approval Stamp).
All maintain the full ECHO Ripple Pulse structure. Emerald #10B981 / #34D399 throughout.
Compare with Passport's chosen QR Portal for consistency in overlay weight.
